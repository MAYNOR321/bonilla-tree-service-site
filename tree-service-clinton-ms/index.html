Local conditions in Clinton

Old water oaks are the ones that surprise people

A water oak can look completely healthy from the street and be hollow through the trunk. They grow fast, get big, and don't live nearly as long as their size suggests, so the ones shading these older streets are frequently past their prime. On a narrow lot with houses on both sides there's nowhere to drop one, which means climbing it and lowering it piece by piece over the roofline. Hinds County is Yazoo clay country too, so the leaning ones aren't leaning by accident.
